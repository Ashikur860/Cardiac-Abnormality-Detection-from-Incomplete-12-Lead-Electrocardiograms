Proposed LAC-SSM Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

Lead-Agnostic and Uncertainty-Calibrated State-Space Modeling for Multi-Label Cardiac Abnormality Detection from Incomplete 12-Lead Electrocardiograms.

📥 Download Full IEEE Journal PDF (47 Pages, 19.8 MB) 📄 View Markdown Manuscript

1. Continuous State-Space Formulation

Continuous-time state-space models represent input signals $u(t) \in \mathbb{R}$ through hidden latent states $x(t) \in \mathbb{C}^N$ governed by linear differential equations:

$$\dot{x}(t) = \mathbf{A} x(t) + \mathbf{B} u(t)$$ $$y(t) = \mathbf{C} x(t) + \mathbf{D} u(t)$$

Where $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{C}^{N imes N}$ is the state-transition matrix initialized with HiPPO (High-Order Polynomial Projection Operators) for long-range temporal memorization.

2. Diagonalization & S4D Discretization

To avoid $O(N^3)$ computational bottlenecks, the proposed architecture restricts $\mathbf{A}$ to a diagonal parameterization $\mathbf{A} = ext{diag}(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_N)$ where $\lambda_n = -\exp(lpha_n) + i eta_n$. Under Zero-Order Hold (ZOH) discretization with step size $\Delta$:

$$ar{\mathbf{A}} = \exp(\Delta \mathbf{A}), \quad ar{\mathbf{B}} = (ar{\mathbf{A}} - \mathbf{I})\mathbf{A}^{-1} \mathbf{B}$$ $$\mathbf{K}_t = 2 ext{Re}\left( \mathbf{C} ar{\mathbf{A}}^t ar{\mathbf{B}} ight), \quad y = u * \mathbf{K} + \mathbf{D} u$$

Convolution over sequence length $T$ executes in $O(T \log T)$ time via Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT), enabling $10 ext{ ms}$ edge inference latency.

3. Dynamic Cross-Lead Attention Pooling

When leads are missing ($m_\ell = 0$), classical CNNs and RNNs experience catastrophic dimensional failure. LAC-SSM utilizes anatomical lead embeddings and softmax masking:

$$z_\ell(t) = ext{LeadStem}(x_\ell(t)) + \mathbf{e}_\ell^{ ext{anat}}$$ $$w_\ell = rac{\exp(\mathbf{W}_{ ext{att}} z_\ell + (m_\ell - 1)\cdot 10^9)}{\sum_{k=1}^L \exp(\mathbf{W}_{ ext{att}} z_k + (m_k - 1)\cdot 10^9)}$$ $$u(t) = \sum_{\ell=1}^L w_\ell \cdot (m_\ell \cdot z_\ell(t))$$

This allows seamless, permutation-invariant aggregation regardless of whether 12, 6, 3, or only 1 single lead is available.

4. Dual Uncertainty Quantification Head

The network outputs both predictive mean logits $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^C$ and heteroscedastic aleatoric log-variance $s = \log \sigma_{ ext{aleatoric}}^2 \in \mathbb{R}^C$. Loss is optimized via attenuated multi-label cross-entropy:

$$\mathcal{L}_{ ext{hetero}} = \sum_{c=1}^C \left[ rac{(y_c - \sigma(\mu_c))^2}{2 \sigma_c^2} + rac{1}{2} \log \sigma_c^2 ight]$$

Epistemic uncertainty $\sigma_{ ext{epistemic}}^2$ is evaluated at runtime via Monte Carlo Dropout over $M=10$ stochastic passes, providing automated clinical triage bounds.
